Used Car Dealer Hub

Follows a car through its whole life on the lot, from listing to after the sale.

Click a screenshot to see it full size.

  • AI writes every car listing, with a template as backup

Tools

n8nClaude (Anthropic)Google SheetsGoogle DriveGoogle CalendarGmail
See how it works

What it does

Staff add a car through a form and AI writes the listing, with a template fallback if AI fails. Buyers fill a public form and get their best matches by email. They can book a test drive, which lands on the dealer's calendar with a reminder the day before. A sale triggers a thank-you plus review, check-in and service emails at 3 days, 30 days and 6 months.

Under the hood

Each car gets a stock number, a photo folder and an AI-written listing, with a template as backup. Buyers are ranked on budget, body type, fuel, age and mileage, and test drives can't be double-booked. Every sale queues follow-ups at 3 days, 30 days and 6 months, and a Monday report flags cars sitting 30+ days.

Construction lead-to-project system

Takes a construction lead from first inquiry to a signed project.

Click a screenshot to see it full size.

  • Every inquiry is scored High, Medium or Low on arrival
  • A daily list of leads overdue for a call
  • PDF quotes drafted for review in one step
  • Accepted jobs get a project folder and tracker row

Tools

n8nGoogle SheetsGoogle DocsGoogle DriveGmail
See how it works

What it does

New leads come in through a form and get a High, Medium or Low score from budget, start date and how complete the inquiry is. Every weekday at 8 AM the salesperson gets a list of leads overdue for a call. Quotes become PDFs from a Google Docs template and wait as Gmail drafts for review. When a client accepts, the system creates the project folder, tracker row and team notice.

Behind the blueprint

Each job inquiry is scored on budget, start date and detail, and gets its own number. Quotes become PDF estimates with revision numbers, and client emails wait as drafts for approval. No job starts without a project manager, duplicates are blocked, and each new job gets a ready-made Drive folder.

Transaction coordination automation

For a realtor client: automating the repeat tasks in my own transaction coordinator work, built so the whole team can run it.

First piece: weekly report process. Every Monday it emails the request for the weekly status report. When the reply arrives, it saves the Excel file to Drive, converts it to a Google Sheet and tells me it's ready. If no report has come in by Tuesday 3 PM, it sends me a reminder.

Second piece: weekly comparator. A custom "Run Weekly Report" menu in Google Sheets rolls last week's report into a history tab and sets up the current week, so the two can be compared.
